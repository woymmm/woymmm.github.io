Privacy Policy for TimeParadox

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Introduction

TimeParadox is developed and published by WOYMMM LTD (“WOYMMM”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). We respect your privacy and aim to keep the game simple and private. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use TimeParadox.

Information We Collect

TimeParadox does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information through the game for storage on WOYMMM-operated servers.

Game Data Stored on Your Device

Gameplay data such as progress, scores, achievements, settings, and other game-related information may be stored locally on your device.

This locally stored data is used only to provide and preserve your game experience. WOYMMM does not operate a server that receives this gameplay data.

Removing the app may also remove locally stored game data, depending on your device and system settings.

Customer Support

If you contact us by email, we may receive information that you choose to provide, such as your email address, name, screenshots, device information, and the contents of your message.

We use this information only to respond to your request and provide support.

Apple Services

If you download TimeParadox through the Apple App Store, Apple may process information related to downloads, device performance, diagnostics, or App Store usage in accordance with Apple's own privacy policies and settings.

Apple may also provide developers with aggregated or diagnostic information through App Store Connect.

Children's Privacy

TimeParadox does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. If a child contacts us for support, any information voluntarily provided will be handled only for the purpose of responding to the support request.

Data Retention

Gameplay data stored locally remains on your device until it is removed by you, by the operating system, or by uninstalling the app.

Support emails and related information are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
