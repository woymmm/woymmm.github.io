When Contacting Us

To help us investigate your issue, please include the following when possible:

  • Your device model (for example, iPhone or iPad model)
  • Your iOS or iPadOS version
  • The version of Solitaire Together you are using
  • A short description of what happened
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue

Game Data

Solitaire Together stores game progress and settings locally on your device. If you uninstall the app, locally stored game data may be removed.
