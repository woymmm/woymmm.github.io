2D Game Artist (Full-time / Freelance)

About Us

We are WOYMMM, an indie game studio passionate about creating unique and engaging experiences. We value creativity and attention to detail, and we are looking for talented artists to help bring our imaginative worlds to life.

Responsibilities

  • Create high-quality 2D game art including characters, environments, props, cards, and UI elements.
  • Collaborate closely with designers and programmers to visually bring game concepts to life.
  • Maintain a consistent art style across all game assets.
  • Independently produce illustrations, concept art, and animation-ready assets.

Requirements

  • Proficient in Photoshop, Clip Studio Paint, Illustrator, or similar 2D art tools.
  • Strong understanding of game art production workflows.
  • Excellent sense of color, composition, and visual storytelling.
  • Experience with card games, strategy games, or indie games is a plus.
  • Good communication skills and ability to work in a collaborative team environment.

Bonus Skills

  • Experience with Spine or Unity 2D animation.
  • A portfolio demonstrating completed games or published work.

What We Offer

  • Creative freedom and an environment that values your ideas.
  • Flexible working options (remote or on-site).
  • Competitive compensation / revenue-sharing for freelance roles.
  • Opportunity to be involved in the full game development cycle, from concept to launch.

How to Apply

Send your resume and portfolio to: info@woymmm.com
Email subject format: 2D Game Artist Application – Your Name
Include your portfolio link in the email body.

Join us and help make our game more vivid and captivating!
