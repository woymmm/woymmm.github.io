Privacy Policy for Solitaire Together

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Introduction

Solitaire Together is developed and published by WOYMMM LTD (“WOYMMM”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). We respect your privacy and aim to keep the game simple and private. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Solitaire Together.

Information We Collect

Solitaire Together does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information through the game for storage on WOYMMM-operated servers.

Game Data Stored on Your Device

Game progress and settings, such as unlocked difficulty levels, achievements, streaks, friendship progress, rewards, language preferences, and audio settings, may be stored locally on your device.

This locally stored gameplay data is used only to provide and preserve your game experience. WOYMMM does not operate a server that receives this gameplay data.

Removing the app may also remove locally stored game data, depending on your device and system settings.

Customer Support

If you contact us by email for support, we may receive the information that you choose to provide, such as your email address, name, screenshots, device information, and the contents of your message.

We use this information only to respond to your request, investigate technical issues, and provide customer support.

Apple Services

If you download Solitaire Together through the Apple App Store, Apple may process information related to downloads, purchases, device performance, diagnostics, or App Store usage in accordance with Apple's own privacy policies and settings.

Apple may provide developers with aggregated or diagnostic information through App Store Connect. Such information is handled by Apple under its own services and privacy terms.

Advertising and Tracking

Solitaire Together does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not use cross-app tracking for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Analytics

Solitaire Together does not include third-party analytics services that collect personal information on behalf of WOYMMM.

Children's Privacy

Solitaire Together does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. If a child contacts us for support, any information voluntarily provided will be handled only for the purpose of responding to the support request.

Data Retention

Gameplay data stored locally remains on your device until it is removed by you, by the operating system, or by uninstalling the app.

Support emails and related information are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain support records, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Your Choices

You control the gameplay data stored locally on your device through your device and app settings.

If you have contacted us for support and would like to ask about the information you provided, you may contact us using the email address below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
